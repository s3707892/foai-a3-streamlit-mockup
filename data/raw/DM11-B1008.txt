Wheel bearing extractor and installer tool that can be used to replaced wheels bearings "ON CAR" without removing the stub assembly and various knuckles that are attached.
This kit will work with any stub assembly that has a CV shaft running through the middle of it, even IRS systems.

NOTE: Must separate wheel hub first to access wheel bearing. Use in conjunction with DM11-C1095-5H
